# Code of conduct

Koi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and to anyone who
represents the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and sexualized or discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to ferrantigiammarco@gmail.com. The maintainers will review every
report, keep the reporter's identity confidential, and respond with the actions described in the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a warning to a permanent ban.
