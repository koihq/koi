# Page checklist

Use the parts that fit the finding. This is a check, not a list of things to add to every page.

## Reachable

- Returns 200 at its canonical URL.
- `robots.txt` allows `Googlebot` and `OAI-SearchBot`, if the owner wants to appear in those engines.
- No unintended `noindex` or `nosnippet`.
- Main content is in the HTML response.
- Linked from a relevant page and listed in the sitemap.

`GPTBot` is a training choice and belongs to the owner. Do not change it as part of a fix.

## Answers the question

- The title and heading match what the buyer asked, in plain words.
- The answer comes early: what it is, for whom, when it fits and when it does not.
- It holds something first-hand: a measured result, a worked example, a limit you know from use.
- Nothing is repeated from another page on the site.

## Evidence

- Each claim has a source, a method or an owner who confirmed it.
- Numbers carry a date and how they were measured.
- Quotes are real, and the person agreed to them.

## Comparisons

- Only facts checked on the competitor's own pages, with a date.
- Says where the competitor is the better choice.
- No insults, and no claims about motives or quality that cannot be shown.

## Identity

- Name, category and one-sentence description match the site, repository and profiles.
- `Organization` markup and `sameAs` point only to real profiles.
- Structured data matches what the page shows.

## Before handing over

- The site builds, links resolve and the project's checks pass.
- The text reads well to a person who has never heard of AI search.
- No page was created just to cover another wording of the same question.
