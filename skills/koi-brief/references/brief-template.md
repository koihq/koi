# Brief template

Leave out a section that has no evidence, and say why in **Limits**.

```markdown
# AI visibility brief: <brand>

Period: <dates> · Engines: <engines> · Runs: <n> · Completed answers: <n>

## Summary

<Two or three sentences: where the brand stands, the main cause, the first thing to do.>

## Where you stand (observed)

| Engine | Cited | Mentioned | Missed | Not usable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Change from the previous period: <only for the same prompts and engines, otherwise "not comparable">

## Who is recommended instead (observed)

| Competitor | Answers | Prompts | Sources cited for it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Why (inferred)

### <Cause>

- Evidence: <prompt> · <engine> · <date> · <source URL>
- Explanation: <why this cause fits>
- Checked: <pages read, or "not verified">

## What to do (recommended)

### 1. <Specific change>

- Addresses: <n> missed answers: <prompts and engines>
- Evidence: <answer and source URLs>
- Where: <file, page or third-party site>
- Change: <what to add, fix or remove>
- Facts needed from you: <claims only the owner can confirm, or "none">
- Practice: <name> (<Official, Research or Hypothesis>), <URL>
- Effort: <small, medium, large> · Confidence: <low, medium, high>
- Done when: <a check on the change itself>
- Recheck: <prompts and engines>, from <date>

## Not worth doing now

- <Tactic>: <why, with source>

## Limits

- <Missing data, pages not verified, small samples>
```

**Done when** describes the change, such as "the page returns 200 and is linked from `/docs`". A
citation in a later answer is the result to watch for, not proof that the change was made.
